I am a Product Manager with 6.5+ years of experience in the payments industry, specializing in merchant acquiring, payment acceptance, EDC terminals, transaction routing, settlement systems, and cross-border payments.

My experience began from engineering and progressed towards business analysis & now progressed into product management, giving me a strong foundation in translating complex business requirements into scalable product solutions. Keeping the key technology stakeholders happy :P I have worked across card acquiring, UPI acceptance, merchant onboarding, payment orchestration, pricing engines, settlement platforms, and ISO 20022 migration initiatives.

Currently, I lead and contribute to products focused on merchant payment acceptance, where I work closely with engineering, operations, acquiring banks, and business stakeholders to design solutions that improve transaction success rates, simplify merchant operations, and drive business growth.

Some of the key initiatives I have worked on include:

* Billing application integration with EDC terminals
* Smart transaction routing and fallback mechanisms
* Consolidated settlement solutions across multiple merchant IDs
* Merchant pricing and rate configuration engines
* Dynamic QR and payment acceptance enhancements
* ISO 20022 and SWIFT payment modernization initiatives

Beyond my day-to-day work, I am actively exploring below topics on which I have done some demo rojects, which are running on the pages linked along with the posts. My current areas of interest include AI/LLM workflows, workflow automations, product strategy, and modern product management practices.

This website serves as a collection of my projects, insights, and learnings from building products in the payments ecosystem. I share thoughts on trends that I am following or ideas I am tinkering with, or on payment infrastructures, product management, and the learnings of AI for perspective of my role.
